MICHI Web Clipper & iOS Setup

Save articles, web links, and ideas into your MICHI Brain Dump from any device.

Option 1: Add to Mobile Home Screen

In Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android), tap the Share icon and select "Add to Home Screen". MICHI will launch as a fast standalone Web Clipper!

Option 2: Safari Bookmarklet

Drag or copy the javascript snippet below into your browser bookmarks bar to clip any active web page in 1 click:

javascript:(function(){var u=encodeURIComponent(location.href),t=encodeURIComponent(document.title);window.open('https://michi-app-five.vercel.app/clipper.html?url='+u+'&title='+t,'_blank');})();
