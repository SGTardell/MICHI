The MICHI (道) Philosophy & 2-Stage Pipeline

Chapter 1

In Japanese culture, 道 (Michi) represents a disciplined journey or path. MICHI organizes your life and work into a streamlined 2-stage creation pipeline:

1. Development Board

Active project work, pending tasks, resource bookmarks, and in-process goals.

2. Completed Board

Finished deliverables, closed projects, archived benchmarks, and milestone records.

Global Header Controls & Color Themes

  • Theme Selector: Switch seamlessly between 🍃 Gentle Breeze (Light) and 冬 MICHI Signature Dark.
  • Project / Plan Selector: Filter your workspace dashboard directly by active Project or Life Plan.
  • Workspace Search: Instantly search notes, appointments, tasks, and bookmarks.

Daily Planner & 2-Page Binder Spread

Chapter 2

The Daily Planner provides an authentic 2-page digital binder spread layout:

  • 3 Mini-Calendars: Side-by-side view showing Previous Month, Active Month (Highlighted), and Next Month.
  • Prioritized ABC Task List: Assign A1, A2, B1, C1 tags to urgent daily objectives.
  • Daily Tracker Box: Log expenses, calls, mileage, or quick thoughts at the bottom left.

Appointment Schedule & 15-Min Precision

  • Dual Time Selectors: Pick exact hour (06 AM–11 PM) and minute increments (:00, :15, :30, :45).
  • Compact Popover Dialogue: Adding a new appointment floats directly over the calendar grid without dark canvas opacity.

Brain Dump Repository, #Hashtags & Web Clipping

Chapter 3

Never lose a fleeting thought, link, or inspiration with the Brain Dump Repository:

  • Zero-Cost #Hashtag Auto-Categorization: Include hashtags like #Tech, #Travel, #Work, or #Personal anywhere in your note or link. MICHI automatically cleans the title and routes the item directly to that topic category tab!
  • Share Sheet Integration: Share web articles, posts, or notes directly into MICHI from any browser or mobile app.
  • Convert to Project: Promote any brain dump idea directly into the Development Pipeline with 1 tap.

Visual Project Boards & Pipeline Advancement

Chapter 4

Organize complex projects visual board style:

  • Development Board: Active projects, progress bars, and item checklists.
  • Advance ➔ Button: Move finished projects seamlessly to the Completed Board with silver mist buttons.
  • Completed Archive: Keep a permanent record of finished projects and deliverables.

Cloud Sync, Password Vault & Backups

Chapter 5

Keep your data secure and accessible across all devices:

  • RESTful Cloud Sync: Real-time background sync keeps your iPhone, iPad, and desktop updated.
  • Password Vault: Secure master-password protected vault for server API keys and login credentials.
  • Backup & Restore: High-visibility action pair in the header allows downloading and restoring full JSON workspace backups.
