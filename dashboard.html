Welcome to MICHI (道)

User Guide & Feature Walkthrough
Step 1 of 5

The 4-Stage Creation Pipeline (道)

In Japanese culture, 道 (Michi) represents a disciplined journey or path. MICHI organizes your ideas into a guided 4-stage pipeline:

1. Spark (Yellow)

Initial ideas, raw visions, and project launch points.

2. Structure (Green)

Web clips, notes, links, and resource bookmarks.

3. Focus (Cyan)

Active tasks, Kanban boards, and daily planner appointments.

4. Product (Purple)

Finished deliverables, credentials, and vault passwords.

Step 2 of 5

Projects & Global Filtering

Group all related ideas, tasks, notes, and credentials under a Project Container:

  • Click + New Project in the top header anytime to launch a new project.
  • Use the 📁 All Projects dropdown on the controls bar to filter your entire workspace grid to a specific project.
  • Visit the 🛤️ Project Lineage view to watch your project evolve across the 4-stage pipeline.
Step 3 of 5

Calendar & Appointment Scheduling

Manage your daily agenda and client schedule effortlessly:

  • Click Calendar in the top navbar to instantly jump to your Monthly Agenda.
  • Tap any date cell on the calendar grid to schedule, edit, or delete appointments.
  • Click + Add Appointment to quickly add a new client appointment or meeting note.
Step 4 of 5

Secure Password & Account Vault

Store web hosting logins, server API keys, and account secrets in your private vault:

  • Click Password Vault in the header to unlock your encrypted records.
  • Set a custom Master Vault Password for maximum protection.
  • Generate strong random passwords with 1 tap using the 🎲 Generate tool.
Step 5 of 5

Disaster Recovery & Data Backup

Never worry about losing your phone or clearing browser data:

  • Click 📥 Backup in the top navbar to download a full JSON backup file of your entire workspace to iCloud Drive or Files.
  • Click 📤 Restore anytime to restore 100% of your projects, appointments, and vault credentials on a new device.
