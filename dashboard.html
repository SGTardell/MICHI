📖 MICHI (道) User Guide & Manual
Chapter 1 The MICHI (道) Philosophy

In Japanese culture, 道 (Michi) represents a disciplined journey or path. MICHI organizes your life and work into a streamlined 2-stage creation pipeline:

1. Development Board

Active project work, pending tasks, resource bookmarks, and in-process goals.

2. Completed Board

Finished deliverables, closed projects, archived benchmarks, and milestone records.

Feature Walkthrough & Tips

Navigate and personalize your workspace effortlessly using top controls:

  • Theme Selector: Switch between:
    • 🍃 そよ風 Soyokaze (Gentle Breeze): Crisp light mode with silver-gray card spreads & teal accents.
    • 冬 Fuyu (MICHI Signature Dark): Deep midnight dark mode with dark navy card spreads & cyan glow accents.
  • 📁 All Projects Filter: Filter your entire dashboard to focus strictly on 1 active project container.
  • 🔍 Workspace Search: Query all tasks, brain dump notes, and project cards instantly.
