MICHI Web Clipper & iOS Setup

Save articles, web links, and ideas into your MICHI Brain Dump from any device.

Option 1: Add MICHI to iOS Share Sheet (iOS Shortcuts)

To make MICHI appear directly in your iPhone's Safari Share Sheet:

  1. Open Apple Shortcuts app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap + -> Name it "Clip to MICHI".
  3. Enable "Show in Share Sheet" in shortcut settings.
  4. Add action: Open URL -> Set URL to:
    https://michi-plum.vercel.app/clipper.html?url=ShortcutInput

Tapping Share in Safari will now show "Clip to MICHI" directly in your Share Sheet!

Option 2: Add to Mobile Home Screen

In Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android), tap the Share icon, scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen". MICHI will launch as a fast standalone Web Clipper app!

Option 3: Safari Favorites Bookmarklet

Bookmark any web page in Safari as "⚡ Clip to MICHI" and replace its address with this 1-click javascript snippet:

javascript:(function(){var u=encodeURIComponent(location.href),t=encodeURIComponent(document.title);window.open('https://michi-plum.vercel.app/clipper.html?url='+u+'&title='+t,'_blank');})();
